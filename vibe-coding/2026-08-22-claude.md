Fixing the code therefore means correcting those two doc statements too.

Before I touch it, one thing genuinely isn't determined by anything in the repo: how the two segments should sit together. Your original samples give `pp. 123-165.` with a closing period, but the DOI form ends with no period at all, so merging them has several plausible readings.
